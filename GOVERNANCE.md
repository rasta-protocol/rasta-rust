# Governance

RaSTA-Rust is an independent project under the `rasta-protocol` GitHub organisation.

Maintainers approve releases and changes to wire behaviour. At least one review
is required before merging a wire-format, state-machine, cryptographic, or
safety-related change. A future assessment baseline may impose stronger review
and independence requirements; the current process does not claim to satisfy a
particular SIL lifecycle.

Architecture decisions are recorded in `docs/adr`. Behavioural inputs are
recorded in `docs/provenance.md`, and requirement observations are kept distinct
from normative requirements.
